# ☕ AURELIA COFFEE CO. — Commercial Finance & Planning Intelligence

> **An interactive Power BI solution for monitoring financial performance, profitability, planning accuracy, commercial investment, trade spend, and pricing performance across brands and sales channels.**

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

**AURELIA COFFEE CO. — Commercial Finance & Planning Intelligence** is a management-focused Power BI dashboard developed for a fictional Sri Lankan coffee and beverage company.

The solution transforms structured commercial finance data into actionable insights, helping management identify **financial performance gaps, profitability drivers, budget variances, forecasting accuracy, revenue leakage, commercial investment priorities, and pricing opportunities**.

---

## 💼 Business Objective

The dashboard is designed to support data-driven financial and commercial decision-making by providing visibility into:

- Sales performance against budgets and forecasts
- Revenue and profitability across brands and sales channels
- Gross Profit and Gross Margin performance
- Operating Profit performance
- Budget vs Actual vs Forecast performance
- Gross-to-Net (G2N) deductions and revenue leakage
- Advertising & Promotion (A&P) investment
- Trade Spend performance
- Product volume and pricing performance

---

## 🔄 Gross-to-Net (G2N) Analysis
